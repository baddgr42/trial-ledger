Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what Trial Ledger stores, why, and how to delete it. Short version: we store only what's needed to calculate ratings, we never sell it, and you can delete it yourself at any time with /delete-my-data.

What we store

  • Your Discord account details: your user ID, username or display name, and a link to your avatar.
  • Servers: the IDs of the Discord servers where you use the bot, to build per-server leaderboards.
  • Match logs you submit: the results you enter (such as escapes, kills and your end-of-match emblem grades), the killer played, the time, and any screenshot you attach. Screenshots stay on Discord; we store only Discord's link to them, which expires after a while.
  • Matches you're tagged in: if another player tags you in a match, we store that you were in it, so it can wait for your stats.
  • Your ratings and record: ratings calculated from your matches, plus counts of undos, flags, crash claims and logging sessions, kept so the rating rules can be applied fairly.

What we don't store

We don't read or store your messages, email address, friends list, or anything else from your Discord account. We don't use cookies or trackers on the website.

Who can see it

Your name, avatar, ratings and match history are public: they appear on the website and in bot replies, such as leaderboards and /rating. Most logging replies are private and visible only to you.

Where it's kept

Trial Ledger runs on Cloudflare, which hosts the bot, website and database. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. Trial Ledger itself does not store IP addresses.

We never sell or share your data

Your data is used only to run Trial Ledger. It is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it, or until Trial Ledger shuts down.

Deleting your data

Run /delete-my-data in any server with the bot. It permanently removes your profile, match logs, ratings, sessions and history, and removes you from matches you were tagged in. Other players' ratings are recalculated without your matches. You can also email trialledger@gmail.com to ask for deletion.

Age

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (older in some countries). Trial Ledger is intended for people who meet Discord's age requirement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

Questions or requests: trialledger@gmail.com.
